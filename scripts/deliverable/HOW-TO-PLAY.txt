THE NORTHLINE RUN
A team game about IF / THEN rules. Ages 8-12. One shared screen.

TO PLAY
Double-click PLAY.bat, or open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Works offline. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.
Fits any screen: laptop, tablet, phone or projector. Turn a phone sideways.

1. BUILD YOUR CREW
   Choose 2, 3 or 4 players. Type your names and pick a face.
   Name the car. Everyone gets the same number of turns.

2. LEARN THE RULE
   A short tutorial shows how a rule works:
   IF the car sees something, THEN it does something.

3. SOLVE EACH SITUATION
   The car stops when it meets something it has no rule for.
   The IF tells you what it sees. Pick the THEN card you think is right.
   Press TEST MY RULE and watch what happens.
   Right: the rule goes into the car's rulebook. Pass to the next player.
   Wrong: the car shows you why. Try again - no one loses a turn.

4. FINISH TOGETHER
   The last delivery is a team run. Then see your team report.

BUTTONS ON SCREEN
   Speaker (top right)  Replay the narration, or mute all sounds.
   i (top right)        How to play.
   Light bulb           A hint and the rulebook.
   Back tab             Pause. Change players, names and faces.

THINKING SKILLS
   Look for the clue. Predict. Test. Fix the rule if it fails.
